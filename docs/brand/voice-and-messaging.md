# DAI Compute - Voice and Messaging

> How the brand speaks and what it says, under the reframing to a Canadian sovereign InfraFi credit protocol. This consolidates the earlier `brand/voice.md` (tone, language patterns) and `brand/brandscript.md` (SB7) and updates the positioning to lead with the two-sided credit market: invest / lend / borrow to build Canadian energy and compute. The detailed tone spectrum and word lists in the original `voice.md` remain in force; this document is the master.

---

## 1. Positioning

**Category:** Sovereign InfraFi. A blockchain protocol for financing Canadian energy and AI compute infrastructure.

**One-liner:** The method for Canadians to invest in, borrow against, and build Canadian energy and AI compute infrastructure.

**The Canada-first brand line:** Blockchain sovereign compute. Built in Canada, backed by Canada, owned by Canadians.

**What we are:** Infrastructure finance with blockchain accounting. A two-sided credit market: depositors earn yield from building Canadian infrastructure; builders borrow against the infrastructure they build.

**What we are not:** A crypto project, a DeFi yield farm, a speculative token. (See prohibited language, Section 6.)

**The differentiator no competitor has:** Sovereignty. USD.AI and every decentralised compute market finance hardware in any jurisdiction. Ours is Canadian by law, backed by Canadian assets, free of CLOUD Act exposure. Sovereignty is the collateral and the moat.

## 3. Two-Sided Messaging (lead with the market)

The site and materials now lead with the **two-sided credit market**. Each side gets its own message:

### To depositors / investors
> Earn yield from building Canadian infrastructure. Deposit into a fully-backed synthetic dollar; stake for yield drawn from real energy and compute loans, not token subsidies. Every dollar is traceable to physical Canadian assets.

### To borrowers / builders (the USD.AI-borrowed concept)
> Non-recourse financing to build Canadian energy and compute. Borrow against the infrastructure you build and the cashflows it generates. Zero-down paths for node operators; institutional facilities for energy companies and data-centre developers.

### To enterprise compute buyers
> Sovereign compute, verifiably Canadian in law. No CLOUD Act. No foreign jurisdiction. Backed by Canadian energy.

### To policy-makers
> Private capital mobilised alongside public investment. A market mechanism that catalyses Canadian-owned sovereign compute at a pace public funding alone cannot achieve.

## 6. Language: Never Use

moon / to the moon, DeFi (as self-label), yield farming, WAGMI / NGMI, ape in, HODL, degen, alpha, number-go-up, Web3 (as primary identifier), guaranteed returns / appreciation, passive income (implies effortless return), get rich.

**Yield discipline:** "yield" is acceptable only when tied to its real source (loan-book interest, T-bill yield). Never imply a guaranteed or speculative return. Never imply CADS or CROWN will appreciate.

## 7. Public-Communication Rules (stealth + pre-CSA)

Because DAI Compute is a stealth-mode flagship and pre-regulatory (CSA Sandbox not yet cleared, no token issued):

1. **No live financial figures on the public site.** Do not publish live APR, TVL, deposit totals, or user counts. USD.AI leads with these; we do not, yet. Present the **model and thesis**; use verified national/infrastructure statistics instead of protocol metrics.
2. **No "deposit now / launch app" CTA.** Use "register interest," "join the cohort," "talk to us." No live financial product is offered until CSA clarity.
3. **Verified data only.** National statistics (TOP500, Budget 2024, energy costs) must trace to primary sources; hedge or omit anything unverified.
4. **Keep sensitive detail off the web.** Financing specifics, the Canada-China dual-campus dimension, partnership names, and any non-public numbers stay in `_journal/`, never on the site or in shared brand materials.
5. **Frame, don't promise.** "Designed to," "structured so that," "the intended mechanism is" — not "you will earn."

When CSA clarity is achieved, a separate revision will enable the live-numbers / launch-app framing.
